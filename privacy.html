1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Zennou ("we," "us," or "the platform") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, community forums, and file hosting services.

Zennou is a platform where creators share, showcase, and document their work — mods, fan projects, patches, and other creative uploads. This policy applies to anyone who visits Zennou, whether or not you create an account.

By using Zennou, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the platform.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what's generated automatically as you use the platform, and what comes from third parties you choose to connect.

Category Examples
Account information Username, email address, password (hashed), profile picture, bio, links you add to your profile
Content you upload Mods, projects, screenshots, videos, changelogs, comments, descriptions
Usage data Pages visited, features used, download activity, timestamps, referring pages
Device & technical data IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
Communications Messages you send us for support, bug reports, or DMCA/legal requests
Third-party login data If you sign in via a connected service (e.g., Discord, Google), we receive basic profile info such as your username and email, as permitted by that service

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain your account and the platform itself
  • Host, display, and distribute the content you choose to publish
  • Show relevant information, such as download counts, follower activity, or comment threads
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to abuse, fraud, malware, or security threats
  • Respond to support requests, bug reports, and legal notices (including DMCA takedowns)
  • Improve platform performance, features, and reliability
  • Send you service-related notifications (e.g., account activity, security alerts) — never unsolicited marketing unless you opt in

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal data, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contractual necessity — processing needed to provide the account and services you signed up for
  • Legitimate interest — such as securing the platform, preventing fraud, and improving our services
  • Consent — for optional features like marketing communications, which you can withdraw at any time
  • Legal obligation — where we're required to retain or disclose data by law

5. How We Share Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Publicly, by your own choice — your username, profile, and published content (mods, comments, showcases) are visible to other users and the public by design, since Zennou is a public creator platform.
  • Service providers — we work with third parties for hosting, storage, analytics, and infrastructure (e.g., cloud storage, CDN providers). These providers only access data necessary to perform their function.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or to respond to valid legal requests (including DMCA notices).
  • Safety & enforcement — to investigate violations of our Terms of Service, protect the rights and safety of Zennou or its users.
  • Business transfers — if Zennou is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used.

  • Essential cookies — required for login sessions and core functionality. These cannot be disabled without affecting the service.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand usage patterns so we can improve the platform. Where required by law, we'll ask for your consent before enabling these.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using certain features.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the platform's services. After you delete your account, we remove personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes (see Section 9 for details specific to account deletion).

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — update inaccurate or incomplete information (most of this is editable directly from your account dashboard)
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data (see Section 9)
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format, where technically feasible
  • Objection / restriction — object to or request we limit certain processing of your data

Most of these actions are available directly from your account dashboard without needing to contact us. For anything not yet available in the dashboard, you can reach out through our support channels.

9. Account Deletion

You are in control of your account. You can delete your account at any time directly from your dashboard settings — no need to submit a manual request or wait for staff approval.

When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, personal information, and account credentials are removed from active systems.
  • You'll be able to choose what happens to content you've published (e.g., mods, comments) — such as removing it entirely, or leaving it visible under an anonymized/deleted-user label, depending on the options available in your dashboard at the time.
  • Some data may be retained for a limited period in backups, or longer where required for legal, security, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes (for example, records related to a DMCA claim or Terms violation).

Our approach: We aim to make account and data deletion as self-serve and transparent as possible. As the dashboard evolves, more granular controls (like deleting individual uploads, download history, or comments) will be made directly available to you, rather than requiring a support request.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure password storage (hashed, never stored in plain text).

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

Zennou is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below this age. If we become aware that we've collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

12. International Users

Zennou is accessible globally. If you access the platform from outside Indonesia, your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Indonesia or other countries where our service providers operate. By using Zennou, you consent to this transfer, consistent with this Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll provide reasonable notice (such as a platform banner or email) before the changes take effect. Continued use of Zennou after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your personal data, can be directed to our support channels, linked in the footer of this site.
