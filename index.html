// 01 — WHY ZENNOU

Built around how creators actually work, not repurposed from a forum template.

Every part follows a real workflow: tinker, ship a version, and talk it through with people who get it.

Version Releases

Ship every release like a Git tag — complete with changelogs, file hashes, and per-version download history.

Structured Showcases

Screenshot galleries, demo videos, and markdown write-ups to present your work the way it deserves.

Creator Profiles

Build a reputation through your contribution history, followers, and community-verified badges.

Threaded Discussions

Comment threads on every project — report bugs, request features, or just show appreciation.

License Control

Set your own distribution terms — from fully open-source to all-rights-reserved, your call.

Flexible Visibility

Publish as a draft, unlisted, or fully public — full control over who sees your work, and when.
