// ABOUT ZENNOU

A home built for people who can't leave a good thing alone.

Zennou exists because creative work deserves a place of its own — not buried in forum threads, not scattered across a dozen file hosts.

// OUR STORY

Why we built this

Every modder, fan developer, and weekend tinkerer knows the feeling: you pour hours into something, and then have nowhere good to put it. Forums bury great work under pages of unrelated posts. File hosts weren't built with creators in mind. And general-purpose platforms rarely understand what it means to ship a "v2.4.1" changelog for something you built purely because you loved the source material.

We started Zennou to fix that. Not as a forum with an upload button bolted on, and not as a file locker with a comments section — but as a platform designed from the ground up around how creators actually work: iterate, release, document, and build a name people recognize.

Zennou is for mods. It's for fangames. It's for the patch you wrote at 2am and the total conversion you've been chipping away at for three years. If you built it because you couldn't stop thinking about how to make something better, this is where it belongs.

// WHAT WE STAND FOR

The values behind every decision we make

These aren't wall art. They shape what we build, what we prioritize, and what we say no to.

Creative Freedom

You should be able to build what you want, document it your way, and own your work — without a platform getting in the way.

Community First

Great creations grow through feedback, collaboration, and people who genuinely care about the craft — not algorithms chasing engagement.

Respect for Craft

Versioning, changelogs, proper documentation — the details matter. We build tools that treat your work with the seriousness it deserves.

Responsible Hosting

We take file safety and intellectual property seriously — for creators, for players, and for the original rights holders whose work inspires so much of what's built here.

Built to Last

We design for the long term — stable infrastructure, clear policies, and a platform that doesn't disappear the moment it stops being trendy.

Honest Communication

Clear terms, clear moderation, and a team that treats creators like collaborators — not just users to be monetized.

// WHERE WE'RE HEADED

Still early. Still building.

Zennou is in open beta — which means the foundation is solid, but we're still shaping what comes next, often based on what creators tell us they need.

PHASE 01

PHASE 02

Creator Tools

Deeper analytics, collaboration features, and more flexible dashboard controls for managing your projects and account.

PHASE 03

Ecosystem Growth

Expanding what Zennou supports — more game integrations, better discovery, and deeper support for teams working on larger projects together.
