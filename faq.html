03

Licensing & Copyright

Yes. You retain ownership of your original work. Uploading to Zennou doesn't transfer any rights to us.

You should avoid redistributing original copyrighted assets — art, models, audio, source code — that you don't own or have rights to. Clearly separate your own original contributions from third-party IP, and respect any modding policy the original rights holder has published.

We follow a standard notice-and-takedown process. If your content is removed following a valid notice, you'll be notified and given the option to file a counter-notice. Full details are in the DMCA Policy.

05

Moderation & Reporting

Every project page has a "Report this project" link. For copyright-specific issues, use the DMCA process instead — it's handled separately from general reports.

Accounts with multiple valid, unrebutted takedown notices against them may be disabled or terminated, in line with our Repeat Infringer Policy.

06

Cost & Fees

Yes. Creating an account, uploading creations, and browsing the platform is free. If paid features are introduced later, they'll be optional and clearly separate from the core experience.

There's no revenue-sharing program at this time. Instead, creators are recognized through platform features — like Verified status once a project crosses certain download milestones — rather than direct payouts.
